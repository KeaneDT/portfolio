Task and Invoice Automation

How the robots shared work

The three robots covered task generation, invoice retrieval and invoice creation. I built the task-generation robot to create work for the others.

Reuse across the robots

I packaged common steps into nine workflows that the robots could call. The team adopted those workflows across the robots. I also prepared five Robot Definition Documents and migrated robots from Kofax RPA to UiPath.
